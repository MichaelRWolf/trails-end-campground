# Registration System & Campground Vision: Values Alignment

This document connects the hybrid registration proposal to the core values embedded in the Vision Signs project. Both reflect the same philosophy about people, presence, and community.

---

## Shared Foundation: Ecology 3.0 & Integration

**Vision Signs:**
> "Instead of separating different activities into isolated zones, we layer them together to create a thriving, sustainable community."

**Registration System Implication:**
The current online registration separates people into pre-assigned zones (like zoning boards). The hybrid system layers people's presence--short-term guests, long-term seasonal residents, workers--into a unified community where everyone claims their place by showing up and looking. No segregation. No leftovers.

---

## Shared Value 1: Direct Experience Over Abstraction

**Vision Signs:**
> "Learn where your food comes from" -- accomplished by walking the farm, seeing the fields, understanding through direct presence.

**Registration System:**
Site selection should work the same way. You can't know what you want from a website description (the farm isn't real until you walk it; the site isn't real until you see it). Choice requires presence and embodied knowledge. Eyes on the land, not clicks on a website.

**The Parallel:**

- Farm: "Here's a description of how we grow. But you need to *visit* to understand."
- Campsite: "Here's a description of the site. But you need to *look* to choose."

Both systems say: **Direct experience is how you know.**

---

## Shared Value 2: Respect for People Who Show Up

**Vision Signs:**
> "A community committed to local food systems" -- the value is in people who *show up* and *participate*.

**Registration System:**
A community that respects presence -- people who drive north, who look around, who participate in real-time. On-site loyalty and commitment should earn respect naturally, not be punished by web-based lockouts.

**The Contrast:**

- Currently: A 1-night online reservation blocks a 90-day on-site camper from a good site.
- Hybrid: On-site presence earns the best available site. Loyalty is respected through action, not rules.

---

## Shared Value 3: Essentials for Thriving

**Vision Signs (Aaron's stated vision):**
> "People need clean water, healthy food, safe living, [community connections] to thrive. When they have these essentials, they can grow, work together, and build strong communities."

**Registration System:**
"Safe living" includes feeling *respected* and *dignified*. When campers feel managed and relegated to leftovers, they don't thrive. When they feel trusted to look around and choose, they thrive. Respect is part of creating the psychological safety Aaron is building toward.

---

## Shared Value 4: Community Over Rules

**Vision Signs:**
> "A community that works together for mutual benefit" -- not through enforcement, but through alignment and trust.

**Registration System:**
The old system worked on trust and good faith ("do the right thing"). The new online system introduced enforcement (you can only use the site assigned to you). The hybrid brings back trust while solving the real problems (cash management, availability certainty).

- Not: "Here's your assigned site. Don't touch anything else."
- Instead: "Pick the best available. We trust your judgment."

---

## Shared Value 5: Layering and Multiple Simultaneous Uses

**Vision Signs (Ecology 3.0):**
> "Instead of separating different activities into isolated zones... layer them together."

**Registration System:**

- Short-term campers (weekend recreation)
- Seasonal residents (sunbirds, workers)
- Long-term campers (90-day stays)
- Maintenance and staff work

All layered together in one campground. The registration system should allow all of these to coexist peacefully:

- Online payment/confirmation (administrative layer)
- On-site selection (human/embodied layer)
- Freedom to move and participate (community layer)

Everyone gets to participate fully, not relegated to pre-assigned categories.

---

## Shared Value 6: Education Through Presence

**Vision Signs:**
> "Educational opportunities about sustainable living" -- learned by being here, looking, participating.

**Registration System:**
Campers learn the campground through presence and exploration, not through a website. The site they choose through direct experience becomes *their* place. They understand the land better. They participate more fully in the community vision.

---

## Summary: Two Systems, One Philosophy

| Dimension                   | Vision Signs                                  | Registration System                             |
|-----------------------------|-----------------------------------------------|-------------------------------------------------|
| **How it works**            | Presence, direct experience, walking the land | Presence, direct experience, looking at sites   |
| **What it values**          | People who show up and participate            | People who show up and participate              |
| **How it builds community** | Shared experience of the land and food        | Shared experience of choosing together, on-site |
| **Key word**                | Integration                                   | Integration                                     |
| **Key word**                | Trust                                         | Trust                                           |
| **Key word**                | Respect                                       | Respect                                         |

Both the vision signs and the hybrid registration system ask: **How do we respect people as whole humans participating in a community, not as categories to manage?**
