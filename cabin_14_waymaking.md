# Signage for Cabin 14

RE: Waymaking past Cabin 14

I love spending time at a site to to observe recurring animal behavior:

- At Site 5, it was PILLEATED WOODPECKERS sequential tree visits.
- At Site 6, it was CHIPMUNKS scurrying into their burrows.
- At Site 1, it was HUMANS repeatedly asking: How big is a 'bundle'?.
- At Cabin 13, it is BICYCLISTS connecting with Orchard Loop and DRIVERS turning around in #14.

Like me, I'm sure some drivers are curious explorers, but I bet that most would appreciate knowing it's a dead end.

In general, I resonate with these famous lyrics...

    Sign, sign, everywhere, a sign
    Blockin' out the scenery
    Breakin' my mind
    "Do this," "Don't do that"
    Can't you read the sign?

... and the "gentle nudges" that Aaron told me that he uses for waymaking on trails, letting people read the lay of the land instead of a sign, but in this case, it feels like a gentle _sign_ beats a gentle _nudge_.

The attached prototype signs incorporated a few design choices:

- For Pedestrians & Bikers
  - Assurance that it's NOT a dead end
  - Top billing (over vehicles) - Indicates First Class status, not common elsewhere
- For Drivers
  - Information to choose: explore or turn around
  - Informative ("Dead End") not Contrilling ("Do Not Enter") signals helpfulness and welcoming
- For Cabin renters
  - Waymaking
- For Nature Lovers
  - Minimal "scenery blocking" (by posting on existing man-made surfaces (trash or toilet))

What do you think?  If you like, I'd be happy to post these until better ones are available.

Hopefully helpful (but not intrusive),
Michael
