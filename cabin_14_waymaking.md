# Signage for Cabin 14

RE: Waymaking past Cabin 14

I love spending time at a site to to observe recurring animal behavior:

- At Site 5, it was a PILLEATED WOODPECKER's evening ritual.
- At Site 6, it was CHIPMUNKS scurrying into their burrows.
- At Site 1, it was HUMANS repeatedly asking: How big is a 'bundle'?.
- At Cabin 13, it is BICYCLISTS zooming by and DRIVERS turning around.

Like me, I'm sure some drivers are curious explorers, but I bet that most would appreciate knowing it's a dead end.

In general, I avoid "Sign, Sign, everywhere a Sign / Blocking out the scenery, breakin' my mind".  I prefer "gentle nudges" that guide people to read the land instead of a sign, but in this case, it feels like a gentle _sign_ beats a gentle _nudge_.

Here's a prototype that trades a few design choices:

- For Drivers - Information to choose: explore or turn around
- For Pedestrians & Bikers - Assurance that it's NOT a dead end
- For Cabin renters - Waymaking
- For Nature Lovers - Minimal visual clutter

What do you think?  If you like, I'd be happy to post these until better ones are available.

Hopefully helpful (but not intrusive),
Michael
