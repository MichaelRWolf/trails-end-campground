# Camper Feedback -- Registration System

Real quotes and observations from campers using the online registration.

## 2026-07-06

**Quote:** "I like the prepaid. My buddy paid for all 3 of us. No shade where we are - 74, 75,76. Couldn't tell that online."

**Context:**

- Likes: prepaid system allows group payments (one person pays for multiple campers)
- Issue: online system doesn't display site characteristics (shade availability) -- had to discover it upon arrival

**Sites:** 74, 75, 76

## 2026-07-07

**Context:**

- Camper with dog arrived; her assigned site had an electric fence (incompatible with dog)
- Assessed the situation immediately with her own judgment (her eyes were sufficient)
- Knew exactly which site she wanted to move to
- Friction: made 2 trips to registration board, had to locate Stener (manager) to authorize the move, required staff to infer the problem
- Self-service system would have eliminated all friction

**Key insight:** Her judgment was sound. All friction came from the app/process (trips, inference, staff involvement), not from decision-making. Visibility + autonomy solve the problem.

Double waste: time lost for camper (2 trips, hunting down Stener) AND time lost for Stener pulled from his work to handle a routine site swap.

**Time:** 5:00 PM
