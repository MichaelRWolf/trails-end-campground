## Messaging Integration: "Soil & Soul" Mission

**Current challenge:** How do we communicate "working farm on the campground" without:

- Making it feel like a petting zoo or attraction
- Overwhelming the sign with text
- Turning exploration into a guided tour

**Options to explore:**

1. **Subtitle/tagline on sign:** "Trails End: Where nature and cultivation meet" (hints at farm without explaining)
2. **Separate small plaque near farm entrance:** "Soil & Soul: Our working farm grows…" (education happens on-site, not at welcome sign)
3. **Map labeling only:** Name the fields, label greenhouses, let the visual tell the story
4. **Silent:** Let guests discover the farm through exploration (Trails End's appeal is discovery, not signage)

**Recommendation:** Option 3 + Option 2 -- the map shows the farm visually and by name; a separate small plaque near the farm entrance tells the story if guests venture there.

## Values-First Messaging (from earlier conversation starters)

### Lead with Why

Structure: Values → Therefore → (minimal rules)

**Draft language:**
> "Trails End sits in one of North America's darkest places, over 5 hours away from nearest Interstate. We value the nature that is so rare, and the natural state of this primative campground that we humans share with the critters and trees and farm.  Help preserve the natural setting by preserving the quiet (i.e. no generators), the dark (i.e. flashlights not porch lights), and the engaging in the "quiet sports" - mountain biking, hiking, kayaking, stargazing.  Let nature set the pace while you are here in this respite from human hardscape, non-stop noise and limitless lights.

### Core Values (choose 3-4 for sign visibility)

- **Quiet & Dark** -- preserve night sky, natural soundscape
- **Movement & Fresh Air** -- hiking, cycling, kayaking, outside time
- **Working Landscape** -- farm, orchard, trails, and wild all together
- **Simplicity** -- we're not a full-hookup park; that's by design
