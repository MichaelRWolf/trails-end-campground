# Registration Board / Signage Project

**Status:** Planning phase

## Context

The transition to online registration requires clear wayfinding and communication at the campground entrance. The welcome/registration sign system should:

- Guide guests to the right place
- Explain the new online process
- Set expectations and tone
- Integrate with broader vision signage system

## Scope

- Welcome sign messaging and placement
- Wayfinding to registration/check-in areas
- Communication of online registration process
- Visual consistency with campground brand

## Key Insights

- Signage supports process flow -- good signs reduce confusion and staff burden
- First impression matters; clear communication sets positive tone
- Opportunities to reinforce campground values and welcome
- Collaborating respectfully with Wendy and stakeholders
