# Registration System Project

**Status:** Planning phase

## Context

Transitioning from first-come/first-serve cash box to online registration system. This shift solves one problem (cash management, fairness, predictability) but introduces new friction points and workflow changes.

## Scope

Document current process flow, identify friction points, and propose solutions for:

- Guest arrival and check-in experience
- Staff workflow and tasks
- Edge cases and exception handling
- Integration with existing campground operations

## Key Insights

- Process flow matters as much as technology
- Small friction points compound over time
- Collaborating with Wendy and stakeholders ensures solutions serve actual needs
