# Right site.  Right sight

## Text

Welcoming a party yesterday, I learned that their online registration experiene was mixed.  They liked pre-paying and pre-guaranteeing and that they cold pre-pay for multiple sites, but were disappointed that pre-selecting a site was a disappointment because they could not know how sunny and open the site was.

I was (uncharacteristically) inspired to poetry

## A poem to a process

Right site.  Right sight.

The new website:
it feels just right
to pay a nightly fee.

I like to feel
behind the wheel
that there's a guarantee.

But when off-site
without my sight -
all sites, they look the same.

A dropdown list
is not the gist
or how to play the game.

That's best on-site -
with sound and sight
and legs to walk around

to see the view
and smells, and feel
the contours of the ground.

The old design:
cash box and sign
could give no guarantee.

The newfound scheme:
an online dream
can never sense a tree.

I dare to say
a hybrid way
is best for everyone.

A guarantee
and pay a fee
is best to do online.

But choose a site
to spend the night
is better done on site.

Technology
used early
to mechanize the flow.

Humanity,
done with a tree,
fits this community.
